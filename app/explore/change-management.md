<p class="govuk-body">REACH is a people-led change management framework. It helps people and organisations collaborate to adopt new ways of working.</p>

<p class="govuk-body">There are many change management frameworks available, and different approaches will suit different teams and organisations. We recommend REACH for helping your teams adopt AI coding tools because it:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>uses a staged approach to help you adapt your support based on what your teams need at each stage</li>
  <li>helps you recognise and welcome resistance to understand what is blocking adoption</li>
  <li>provides teams with targeted support to comfortably move beyond resistance</li>
  <li>ensures individual actions and organisational support progress at the same pace</li>
</ul>

<p class="govuk-body">Our change leads work with AI champions and sponsors to provide tailored support at each stage of the adoption journey.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-l">Explore the Knowledge Hub</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">When you begin working with AI Engineering Lab, your team will get access to the Knowledge Hub. This is a space to:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>learn about the REACH framework and access resources</li>
  <li>access a calendar of events and join training sessions</li>
  <li>engage with and learn from peers in other organisations</li>
</ul>

<p class="govuk-body">You need a government or public sector email address to sign up. Personal email addresses are not accepted.</p>

<p class="govuk-body"><a href="https://khub.net/group/ai-engineering-lab/group-home" class='govuk-link' target='_blank' rel='noopener noreferrer'>Access the Knowledge Hub</a>.</p>
